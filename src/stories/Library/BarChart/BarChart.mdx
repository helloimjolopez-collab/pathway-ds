The columns a Detail or Explore widget fills with. One Figma set holds both this
and Mini Chart: the Bar types map here, the Line types map there.

**The three bands in a column overlap.** Each band runs from the baseline to its
own total and has a rounded top, so the one above shows as a cap above the one
below. Drawn as stacked segments instead, each sitting on top of the last, the
middle band is a floating block with a rounded top in mid-air and the bottom band
loses its curve entirely.

**Two colours alternate, not three.** The ramp steps are `09` and `11`, with `07`
available for a third series. They come from the dedicated fifteen-step chart
ramp and not from the blue brand ramp, because the bars are categories and
nothing in them is good or bad. An earlier version bound `Fill/Static/Brand` and
came out navy where the design is violet.

## Anatomy

```
BarChart
├── y title            rotated, 18 wide
├── plot
│   └── column         one per stack, gap = Gap/Tight
│       ├── band 07    baseline to total, r=8 top
│       ├── band 09    baseline to its own total, r=8 top
│       └── band 11    baseline to its own total, r=8 top
├── axis line          1px, bottom
├── x title
└── legend             one swatch per named series
```

The cap radius is 8 and it is applied to the top corners only. A zero-height band
with a radius on it draws a 1px sliver with a curve that cannot fit, which is
visible as a smear along the axis; the implementation drops the radius below the
height that can carry it.

Axis titles are props rather than always-on, because the same chart is used at
112px wide inside a KPI tile, where a rotated y title is wider than the plot.

## States

<Canvas of={Stories.StateMatrix} />

A chart's states are its data's states. Each of the five shown here drew wrong at
some point: a single column stretched to the full width, a single series lost the
alternating ramp, a zero column drew the smear described above, and an empty
series drew an axis with nothing against it.

## Responsive behaviour

The plot fills its container on both axes and the column gap is a token rather
than a percentage, so a narrower chart gets narrower columns and keeps its
rhythm. Below roughly 240px the axis titles should be dropped by the parent: the
component does not drop them itself, because only the parent knows whether the
information is available elsewhere on the card.

## Accessibility

| Concern | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| The plot | `aria-hidden="true"`, with the chart's own `label` prop announced in its place. |
| Series identity | Carried by the legend's text, not by colour alone. |
| Contrast | The three ramp steps are chosen for separation against each other as well as against the surface, which is why they are not three steps in a row. |
| Reduced motion | Nothing animates. |
