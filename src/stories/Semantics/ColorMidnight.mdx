# Semantic Color — Midnight Mode

Pathway's dark mode is called **Midnight Mode**. The name matters in both directions:
it is the Pathway name in the Figma Variables panel and in `themes/midnight.css`, and
it is the only name used in any spec or document. The single exception is the CSS
selector, which matches both `[data-theme="midnight"]` and `[data-theme="dark"]`, so a
consumer on another team does not have to learn our vocabulary to switch themes.

These are the **same 326 names** as Light Mode, carrying different values. That is the
point of the contract: a component names `--semantic-color-foreground-static-neutral-bold`
once and both modes resolve it by selector. There is no mode-in-name form any more —
`--semantic-color-dark-mode-*` came from `tokens.css`, retired 2026-09-03, and now
resolves to nothing.

On the graded ladder the **values invert while the names hold**: Faint stays the
lightest-touch step and Bold the heaviest, even though in Midnight that means Faint is
a dark near-ground and Bold is near-white. White, XLight and Black are inversion
anchors, so they keep their value across modes and are marked `anchor` below.

Contrast badges are measured against a black ground, which is what Midnight surfaces
resolve to.
